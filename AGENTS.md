# Watershed OSINT operating instructions

Read `README.md`, the maintained evidence model and the latest dated review before changing an assessment. The user's standing preference is to use OpenStreetMap, public data and standard process expectations to make useful best guesses when exact facility information is unavailable.

- Put those guesses in `data/facility-hypotheses.json` as **INFERRED**, with qualitative confidence, specific evidence basis, alternative explanations, review dates and a way to verify or disprove them. Do not leave every likely ingredient or equipment category blank simply because the exact purchase list is private.
- Separate advertised company capabilities, site/process allocation, likely process inputs, actual procurement and installed equipment. Do not infer named suppliers, customers, equipment makes/models, capacities, inventories or utility providers as facts. Hypothetical quantities need explicit scenario assumptions and must stay separate from measured company exposure.
- Preserve CONFIRMED / HISTORICAL / INFERRED classes through graph traversal. Repetition cannot upgrade a claim. A planned expansion does not establish commissioning, and a contact address does not establish manufacturing there.
- Use cached OpenStreetMap municipal references and regional corridors. Keep ODbL attribution. The five saved points are not plant coordinates; Milwaukee is a regional reference, not the confirmed Wisconsin plant municipality. Nearby roads/rail do not establish company use, legal truck access or independent alternatives. Do not map sensitive facility-security details.
- Refresh fixed Overpass extracts only on their catalog cadence and review mapping changes. Do not add a generic live Nominatim geocoder. If a specific new place needs a lookup, follow the linked OSM usage policy, identify the application, use one client, rate-limit and cache the result.
- Government/issuer measurements are benchmarks, not actual Watershed costs or orders. Preserve release date, reference period, units, SA/NSA treatment and preliminary status. BLS PPI table 2 annual changes are NSA even when the monthly row is SA.
- Keep failed/blocked collectors visible. Use documented public alternatives or independently dated primary-source reads; do not bypass access controls. Discovery candidates never automatically become scored assessments.
- Apply the existing risk equation with explicit factor rationales. Shared economic mechanisms use one issue group; S01/S13 share fuel cost. Keep Cyber / OT unassessed until applicability can be matched, while maintaining general dependency hypotheses.
- Select executive personas from the current evidence, retain stable advice when appropriate, and preserve genuine daily history. Do not manufacture yesterday's scores or resolve forecasts before their outcome windows close.
- Update source citations, hypothesis counterevidence, gap-resolution steps and the dated review alongside the analysis. Run the parser/model tests and type checking for relevant code changes. Preserve private analyst overrides and the dashboard's existing audience.
